## Narrative spine

The page is built on a five-element sales narrative. Every section on the page serves one element. If a section does not serve an element, it does not belong on the page.

| # | Element | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Name a big, relevant change in the world | Scroller chapter 01 |
| 2 | Show there'll be winners and losers | Scroller chapter 02 |
| 3 | Tease the promised land | Scroller chapter 03 → `#how` |
| 4 | Introduce features as "magic gifts" for overcoming obstacles | `#modules` · `#offers` · `#faq` |
| 5 | Present evidence you can make the story come true | `#process` · `#compliance` · `#pricing` · `#team` |

**The change we name:** businesses here now grow by opening branches, but business software still assumes one location is one company. Growth turned into a visibility problem.

**Why this change and not another:** it is the one the buyer has already lived. We are not teaching them a trend; we are naming what already happened to them. The two rejected alternatives — "the verification era" and "AI made custom software cheap" — both require the buyer to accept a premise first.

**The promised land is not our product.** It is: *every branch's numbers by 9am, and nobody retyping a report.* The Operating Layer is how they get there.

**Page title (≤60):** `Axon Enjin — One System for Every Branch`

**Meta description (≤155):**
`Operating Layer for multi-branch businesses in the Philippines. Start empty, add modules, or get custom AI software. We work like your in-house developers.`

**H1 (real DOM, sr-only):** `Axon Enjin builds the Operating Layer — one system for every branch.`

**Primary keywords:** operating layer · multi-branch business software Philippines · franchise management software Philippines · modular ERP Philippines · custom AI software Philippines · software for multiple branches

**AISO principles applied on the page:**

1. **Answer-first sentences.** Every FAQ answer states the answer in the first sentence, before any qualification. Language models extract the first sentence.
2. **Self-contained claims.** No answer depends on a previous answer to make sense. Each block survives being quoted alone.
3. **Explicit negatives.** "No, we do not accept payments." A stated negative is retrievable; an omission is not. This also doubles as our compliance posture.
4. **Named entities in plain text.** Axon Enjin, Metro Manila, Philippines, the four people, the seven modules, the three offers — all appear as literal text, not only as images or canvas.
5. **Structured data mirrors visible copy.** Nothing in JSON-LD that a visitor cannot read on the page.
6. **`llms.txt` carries the same claims in the same words** as the page. Divergence between the two reads as unreliability.

## FAQ copy *(Element 4 + primary AISO asset)*

Answer-first. Each answer must survive being quoted with no surrounding context.

**What is an Operating Layer?**
It is one system that every branch works in. You start with an empty Core — login, branches, roles, home screen, billing, and an activity log. Then you buy modules, and each one unlocks a new tab in the same system.

**Do I have to replace my POS?**
No. Keep the POS you already use. We connect to it.

**Do I need to buy every module?**
No. You start empty and buy one. Add the rest when you need them.

**What if it does not fit how we work?**
We change it. Offer 2 tailors fields, workflows, and extra tabs on top of the catalog. Offer 3 is new software built for you from scratch.

**Does this make my business BIR compliant?**
No. You hold your tax registrations and you file. We build software. We do not claim BIR or BSP accreditation we do not hold. The Accounting module keeps management books and reports, and exports or syncs to the accounting system you already register with the BIR.

**Can you accept payments for us?**
No. Money does not move through us. Your payment gateway accepts payments, and we connect your Operating Layer to it.

**Who owns the software?**
On a custom build, you own the app you paid for. Axon Enjin keeps the reusable libraries and general patterns we use across projects.

**How long does it take?**
About four weeks for modules, about three months when we customize, and about four months for custom AI software plus twelve months of care. Real dates come with your proposal.

**What does maintenance cover?**
Defect fixes inside the agreed scope, security and dependency updates we control, and support by message with scheduled check-ins. It does not cover new features, new modules, or changes forced by an outside service we do not control.

**How much does it cost?**
We do not publish prices. Numbers come in a written quote once we know how many branches you run and which modules you need.

**Who is this for?**
Businesses in the Philippines that run more than one branch, including franchise networks. Custom AI software is open to any business, even a single location.

**Where are you based?**
Metro Manila, Philippines. We are a team of four and we work with businesses across the Philippines.

## Hard don'ts

- Any peso or dollar figure anywhere on the site — prices live only in written quotations (F-34)
- "Franchise Operating Layer" or "FOL" as the page title; franchise is a use case, not the product name
- Readiness Audit as hero CTA
- Starter / Growth / Enterprise tables
- Pure-equity / "build for free" language
- "Fully BIR compliant", claimed accreditation, or payment acceptance as our capability
- Accounting as a full CAS replacement
- Ventures in the primary nav or as a hero CTA
- Invented customers, logos, testimonials, headcount, or market-size statistics
- Urgency theatre, countdowns, growth-hacking tone. The buyer is a family business with twenty years of operating history.
- Quantifying a client's tax deduction — flag the incentive, never do the arithmetic
