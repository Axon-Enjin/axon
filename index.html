About us

The one
who connects.

Axon means axis — a cable that carries a signal. Enjin means engine: the one who connects. We connect people through systems.

We are an operating company first. We build software. We work like in-house developers. We sometimes co-build startups. That second path is smaller, and we say so.

We do not claim BIR or BSP accreditation we do not hold. You keep your registrations. You file.

Money does not move through us. Your own payment tools accept payments.

We do not list work we cannot name. We do not dress Year-0 as a large studio.
