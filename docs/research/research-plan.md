> into captured commercial value — across positioning, price architecture, buyer

## The 9 Pillars

### Wave 1 — Foundational

**P1 — Value Capture & Price Architecture**
Value-based and outcome pricing; cost-floor math (fully-loaded rate, billable
utilization); tiering and anchoring; expedited premiums; risk premiums;
retainer benchmarks; pass-through infra treatment.
*Formulas wanted:* break-even day rate, ROI-multiple test, retainer as % of
build, blended margin, LTV/CAC.

**P2 — Buyer Selection & Qualification**
The multi-branch thesis (pain × locations ÷ single decision chain); ICP scoring
models; "conscious loss" signals (coping spreadsheets, a hired reconciler, a
prior quote); disqualifiers; economic-buyer vs technical-buyer mapping.

**P3 — Positioning & Category**
Category design ("Operations Transformation Partner"); specialization vs
generalist economics; credibility substitutes when you have no logos; offshore
trust objections and their antidotes.

### Wave 2 — Depends on Wave 1

**P4 — Demand Generation & Offer Design**
Free-work-as-marketing economics and failure modes; scarcity and selection
framing; content sequencing; lead-magnet → discovery-call mechanics;
market-intelligence harvesting from applicant pools.

**P5 — The Sales Conversation**
Discovery and diagnostic frameworks; quantifying cost of inaction; ethical
loss-aversion framing; three-option presentation; objection library;
never-quote-in-the-room discipline; deferring timeline commitments.

**P6 — Delivery Discipline as Margin Defense**
Scope definition; change-order process; revision limits; estimation under
uncertainty; buffer vs sandbag; delivery guarantees;
expectation-versus-satisfaction mechanics.

### Wave 3 — Synthesis & Constraints

**P7 — Productization & Reuse Economics**
Vertical templates; % reuse as margin engine; IP ownership models; module
libraries; the "fast because we've built it fourteen times" attribution.

**P8 — Geography & Market Arbitrage**
AU / NZ / PH / US / Middle East on rate reference, timezone, trust hurdle,
sales cycle; offshore price positioning (why undercutting reads as low quality);
data residency; entity, tax, invoicing.

**P9 — Commercial & Legal Instruments (PH-first)**
SOW, MSA, deposits and milestones, IP-on-final-payment, liability caps,
abandonment clauses, testimonial consent, Data Privacy Act.
**Awareness only — disclaimer mandatory.**

## Output Contract (every pillar returns this shape)

1. **Pillars** — load-bearing principles
2. **Frameworks** — named, multi-step, reusable
3. **Techniques** — situational tactics, each with its trigger condition
4. **Hacks** — asymmetric low-effort/high-leverage moves
5. **Terms** — glossary, one line each
6. **Formulas** — literal expression + variable definitions + worked PHP/AUD example
7. **Benchmarks** — numbers with date stamp and source tier
8. **Contradictions** — sources disagreeing, stated as open questions, NOT resolved
9. **Sources** — tier-labeled (primary / practitioner / journalistic / web)

## Standing Rules

- Report and cite. Do not recommend. Synthesis happens later.
- Preserve contradictions. Flattening them destroys the most valuable signal.
- P4 and P5: mark every persuasion technique **legitimate** / **grey** /
  **misrepresentation**. The system must survive a client asking who is on the project.
- P9: append *"Structural and regulatory awareness only — not legal advice.
  Consult a licensed Philippine attorney."*
- Note source tier and retrieval date on every finding.
