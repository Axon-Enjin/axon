# Axon Enjin — 7-Day MSME Ad Campaign Plan

## Campaign Name
# AXON ENJIN BUILD SPRINT

---

# Campaign Objective

## Primary Goal
Position Axon Enjin as:
- fast execution builders,
- problem solvers for MSMEs,
- operational system specialists,
- and a startup capable of delivering real business impact.

---

# Core Campaign Narrative

> “We spent months proving ourselves in hackathons and technical competitions.
Now, we want to build solutions for real businesses.”

Axon Enjin is launching a 7-Day Build Sprint Campaign dedicated to helping underserved MSMEs improve operations through sponsored MVP systems and software solutions.

---

# Primary Audience

## Target Market
### MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises)

### Why MSMEs?
MSMEs:
- operate with manual systems,
- are underserved technologically,
- often cannot afford enterprise software,
- experience operational inefficiencies daily,
- and need practical systems more than complex innovation.
