# Axon Enjin — 7-Day Build Sprint Campaign Plan

## Campaign Overview

### Campaign Name
**Axon Enjin Build Sprint**

### Core Narrative
Axon Enjin has spent months proving its capability through hackathons, technical competitions, and rapid execution as Iskolar ng Bayan builders from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.

Now, the mission evolves.

Instead of building only for competitions, Axon Enjin will dedicate one week to solving real-world business problems through sponsored MVPs and rapid software solutions for selected businesses.

This campaign is designed to:
- Generate qualified leads
- Gather real-world business problems
- Build portfolio-worthy systems
- Acquire testimonials and case studies
- Establish authority and credibility
- Create future client pipelines
- Strengthen Axon Enjin’s brand presence

---

# Strategic Objective

## Primary Goals
1. Acquire high-quality leads
2. Build real-world case studies
3. Create strong testimonials
4. Expand network and business relationships
5. Gather market intelligence
6. Position Axon Enjin as rapid execution specialists

---

# Campaign Structure

## Offer
Selected businesses will receive a:
- Sponsored MVP
- Prototype solution
- Internal business tool
- AI-assisted workflow system
- Dashboard or automation system

## Important Positioning
DO NOT market this as:
> “Free Custom Software”

Instead market it as:
> “Sponsored MVP Build Sprint”

This creates:
- exclusivity
- professional positioning
- expectation boundaries
- controlled scope

---

# Application Form Fields

## Basic Information
- First Name
- Last Name
- Position
- Company Name
- Industry
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- City
- Country

## Business Discovery
- What is your biggest operational problem solvable by software or AI?
- What repetitive task consumes the most time in your business?
- What are the top 3 features you wish your ideal system had?
- How is your team currently handling this process?
- What happens if this problem remains unsolved?

---

# Selection Criteria

## Ideal Candidates
Prioritize businesses that:
- Have repetitive manual workflows
- Have clear operational pain points
- Can be solved quickly
- Have strong testimonial potential
- Can become long-term clients

## Avoid
Avoid:
- vague startup ideas
- enterprise-level complexity
- unlimited scope requests
- indecisive founders
- people expecting a full SaaS platform

---

# Scope Protection

## Project Limits
Every selected business must agree to:
- Maximum of 3 core features
- MVP/prototype only
- No native mobile apps
- No advanced enterprise integrations
- Limited revision cycle
- Timeline limitations
- Hosting limitations
- Maintenance not included unless agreed separately

---

# Catch / Conditions

## Terms
1. Hosted under Axon Enjin infrastructure unless client provides their own
2. Paid third-party services handled by the client
3. Axon Enjin watermark/footer attribution included
4. Finished project added to Axon Enjin portfolio
5. Client provides testimonial upon completion
6. Major scope expansion converts into paid engagement

---

# Legal Protection Requirements

## Must Prepare Before Launch
- Scope Agreement
- MVP Definition
- Revision Limits
- Timeline Agreement
- Testimonial Consent
- Liability Limitation
- Abandonment Clause
- Hosting Responsibility Clause
